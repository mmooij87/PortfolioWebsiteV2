What I Do

I create high-quality 3D animations, managing the entire process from concept to final delivery. Specializing in 3D animation, visualization, and physics simulations, I ensure each project is both technically precise and visually striking. By staying current with the latest software developments, I craft contemporary animations that align with today’s standards and expectations.

Who I Am

I’m a Motion Graphic Designer from the Netherlands, specializing in 3D animations, visualizations, and physics simulations. With a master's degree in Architecture and over a decade of experience in the rendering industry, I combine technical expertise with creativity. I’m passionate about turning complex technologies into engaging visual stories.
